Files are trimmed, converted to mono MP3 and renamed `<kind>_<n>.mp3`; the game picks a random
variant of a kind each time it plays one.

## Animal calls, foley and ambience

Each clip was trimmed, converted to mono 44.1 kHz MP3, high-passed at 40 Hz, faded and peak-normalised; the ambience beds were made to loop seamlessly.
