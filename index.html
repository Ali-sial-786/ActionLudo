ACTION LUDO 🧸 Kids

Classic Ludo rules. Apex predators. Every capture is a fight to the death — then the carcass is chained and dragged back to its cage.

Roll the dice, race your animal pals home, and bonk your friends back to their cozy cages!

How to play
  • Every animal starts locked in a cage. Roll a 6 and a golden key unlocks a cage, releasing the animal onto your start square.
  • Move clockwise around the board, then up your coloured home column to the centre. You need the exact roll to get home.
  • Land on a lone opponent and the two clash: mash any key or tap to win. If the attacker wins, the loser is torn apart, chained, and dragged back to its cage; if the defender wins, the two share the square.
  • Star squares and start squares are safe, and so are two pieces sharing a square.
  • Rolling a 6, capturing, or getting a piece home earns another roll. Three 6s in a row forfeits the turn.
  • First to bring all four animals home wins. To roll, grab the die and fling it (or hold Space). Change any of this under House rules.
  • Your four animal pals start in their cozy cages. Roll a 6 and a magic golden key lets one out onto your start square. 🔑
  • Hop around the board, then up your own coloured path to the middle. You need the exact number to get home.
  • Land on a friend's square and it's a bonk-off: tap tap tap! Win it and your animal does its silly move — bonk! — and they float back to their cozy cage. 🎈 Lose it and you share the square.
  • Star squares ⭐, start squares and buddies sharing a square are safe spots: no bonking there.
  • Rolling a 6, bonking a friend, or getting home means you roll again! Three 6s in a row skips your turn.
  • First to get all four pals home wins! Grab the die and toss it to roll. Change the rules under House rules.
