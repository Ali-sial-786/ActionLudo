ACTION LUDO

Classic Ludo rules. Apex predators. Every capture is a fight to the death — then the carcass is chained and dragged back to its cage.

How to play
  • Every animal starts locked in a cage. Roll a 6 and a golden key unlocks a cage, releasing the animal onto your start square.
  • Move clockwise around the board, then up your coloured home column to the centre. You need the exact roll to get home.
  • Land on an opponent on a normal square and your animal attacks — the loser is torn apart, chained, and dragged back to its cage.
  • Star squares and start squares are safe: no fighting there.
  • Rolling a 6, capturing, or getting a piece home earns another roll. Three 6s in a row forfeits the turn.
  • First to bring all four animals home wins. Click the hovering die (or press Space) to roll; drag to orbit the camera.
